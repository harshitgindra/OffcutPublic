# Privacy Policy

**Offcut — Cut Layout Optimizer**
*Last updated: May 14, 2026*

## Overview

Offcut is a local-first utility app that helps woodworkers optimize cut layouts for sheet materials. Your privacy matters to us — this policy explains what data Offcut collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

---

## 1. Data We Collect

**Offcut does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers owned or operated by us.**

All project data (sheets, pieces, layouts, settings) is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework, or synced privately through your personal iCloud account if you have iCloud enabled.

### 1.1 Data You Provide

When you use Offcut, the following data is created and stored **locally on your device only**:

- Project names, dimensions, and cut list data you enter
- Sheet material dimensions and quantities
- Tool profile selections and settings
- App preferences (unit system, default kerf, etc.)

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it (see Section 3).

### 1.2 iCloud Sync (Optional)

If you have iCloud enabled on your device and grant Offcut permission, your project data may be synced across your devices via **Apple's iCloud (CloudKit)** service. This sync:

- Is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- Is end-to-end encrypted between your own devices
- Is never accessible to us
- Can be disabled at any time in **Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Offcut**

### 1.3 In-App Purchases

Offcut offers optional paid features via Apple's App Store. Purchase transactions are processed entirely by **Apple** and governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We do not receive your payment information. We receive only a confirmation from Apple's StoreKit framework that a purchase has been made, which is stored locally on your device to unlock features.

### 1.4 App Store Diagnostics

If you opt in to sharing analytics with Apple, Apple may collect anonymous usage and diagnostic data about the app. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy and is not accessible to us. You can control this in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements**.

---

## 2. Data We Do NOT Collect

- No account registration or login required
- No analytics or usage tracking by us
- No crash reporting to third-party services
- No advertising identifiers (IDFA or IDFV)
- No location data
- No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
- No network requests to our servers of any kind
- No behavioral profiling or cross-app tracking

---

## 3. Data Sharing

Offcut only shares data when **you explicitly initiate** a share action:

- **PDF Export**: When you tap the export/share button, a PDF is generated locally on your device and presented through Apple's standard share sheet. You control where the PDF goes (AirDrop, Files, email, Messages, etc.).
- **CSV Export**: If you export a cut list as CSV, it is generated locally and shared via the system share sheet under your control.

No data is shared automatically or in the background.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

Offcut does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection SDKs. The only external services involved are:

| Service | Purpose | Governed By |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Apple App Store | App distribution & in-app purchases | [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) |
| Apple iCloud (CloudKit) | Optional cross-device sync | [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) |

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

All data stored by Offcut resides on your device. You have full control:

- **Delete a project**: Permanently removes that project's data from your device (and iCloud if sync is enabled).
- **Delete the app**: Removes all app data from your device permanently. iCloud data can be deleted separately via **Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Offcut**.
- **Reset all data**: You can clear all app data from within the app Settings screen.

We do not retain any copies of your data on our servers because we do not have access to it.

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights including:

### California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)
California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. **Offcut does not sell personal information.** Since we do not collect personal data, most CCPA requests are not applicable. For any questions, contact us at the email below.

### European Residents (GDPR)
If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation including the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data, and the right to data portability. Since all data is stored locally on your device and we do not process your personal data, you can exercise these rights directly by managing or deleting the app. For questions, contact us at the email below.

### Other Jurisdictions
We respect applicable privacy laws in all jurisdictions. If you have questions about your rights, contact us.
