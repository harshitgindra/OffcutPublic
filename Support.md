# Offcut Support

**Offcut — Cut Layout Optimizer**

Welcome to Offcut support. We're here to help you get the most out of your cut layout planning.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**Q: How do I create my first project?**
Tap the **+** button on the Projects screen. The New Project Wizard will guide you through three steps: naming your project and selecting your material type, adding your sheet stock dimensions, and entering the pieces you need to cut.

**Q: What units does Offcut support?**
Offcut supports both **Imperial** (inches, fractions) and **Metric** (millimetres) units. You can switch units in **Settings → Unit System**. Fraction input (e.g. 3/4", 1-3/8") is fully supported on the Imperial keyboard.

**Q: How many projects can I have?**
The free tier supports **1 project**. Upgrading to the **Hobbyist** tier unlocks unlimited projects.

### Cut Layout Optimization

**Q: What algorithm does Offcut use?**
Offcut uses the **MAXRECTS** bin packing algorithm with multiple heuristics, combined with guillotine packing for tool-constrained cuts. It generates up to 8–10 distinct layout options so you can choose the one that best fits your workflow.

**Q: Why did some of my pieces get dropped from the layout?**
If all your pieces cannot fit on the sheets you've provided, Offcut will drop lower-priority pieces to produce a valid layout. Dropped pieces are shown in the results with an explanation. To fix this, try:
- Adding more sheets
- Increasing sheet quantity
- Reducing piece dimensions
- Upgrading piece priority

**Q: What does piece priority mean?**
- 🔴 **Critical** — Never dropped; Offcut will add extra sheets if needed
- 🟠 **High** — Placed before Normal/Low priority pieces
- 🟡 **Normal** — Standard priority (default)
- 🟢 **Low** — Dropped first when space is tight

**Q: How do I set a tool profile?**
Go to **Settings → Tool Profile** and select the cutting tool(s) you have available. Offcut adapts the layout algorithm based on your tools — for example, a Table Saw uses guillotine cuts only, while a Jigsaw allows free-path cutting.

**Q: What is grain direction locking?**
When enabled for a piece, grain direction locking prevents the piece from being rotated 90°. Use this for pieces where the wood grain must run in a specific direction (e.g. cabinet doors, visible panels).

### PDF Export

**Q: How do I export my layout as a PDF?**
From the Layout Results screen, tap the **Share / Export** button. A PDF containing your cut diagrams and parts legend will be generated locally on your device and presented via the standard iOS share sheet.

**Q: PDF export is not available — why?**
PDF export requires the **Hobbyist** tier or higher. The free tier supports screenshot export only.

### iCloud Sync

**Q: How do I enable iCloud sync?**
iCloud sync is available on the **Prosumer** and **Pro** tiers. Once subscribed, ensure iCloud is enabled for Offcut in **iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Offcut**.

**Q: My projects aren't syncing across devices — what should I do?**
1. Confirm you're signed in to the same Apple ID on both devices
2. Confirm iCloud is enabled for Offcut (Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Offcut)
3. Ensure both devices have an active internet connection
4. Try force-quitting and reopening the app on both devices

### Purchases & Subscriptions

**Q: How do I restore my purchases on a new device?**
Go to **Settings → Restore Purchases** within the app. Your purchases are tied to your Apple ID and will be restored automatically.

**Q: How do I cancel a subscription?**
Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time through your Apple ID account:
1. Open **iOS Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions**
2. Tap **Offcut**
3. Tap **Cancel Subscription**

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

**Q: How do I request a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) to submit a refund request.

**Q: I was charged but my upgrade isn't showing — what should I do?**
Go to **Settings → Restore Purchases** in the app. If the issue persists, contact us at the email below with your Apple Order ID (found in your App Store purchase confirmation email).

### Data & Privacy

**Q: Does Offcut collect any of my data?**
No. Offcut does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All your project data stays on your device. See our [Privacy Policy](PrivacyPolicy.md) for full details.

**Q: How do I delete all my data?**
You can delete all data by deleting the app from your device. This permanently removes all project data from local storage. If iCloud sync is enabled, you can also remove iCloud data via **iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Offcut → Delete Data**.

### Troubleshooting

**Q: The app crashed or is behaving unexpectedly**
Try the following steps:
1. Force-quit the app (swipe up from the app switcher) and reopen it
2. Restart your device
3. Ensure you are running the latest version of Offcut (check the App Store for updates)
4. Ensure your device is running iOS 17 or later

If the issue persists, please contact us with a description of the problem.

**Q: What iOS version is required?**
Offcut requires **iOS 17.0 or later**.

**Q: Is Offcut available on iPad?**
Yes, Offcut runs on iPad. Full iPad split-view support is planned for a future update.

**Q: Is Offcut available on Mac?**
Not currently. Offcut is an iOS/iPadOS app only at this time.
